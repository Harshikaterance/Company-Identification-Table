# Company-Identification-Table
## Company Identification & Share Price Analysis

This repository contains Excel-based financial analysis projects, including company identification data and share price analysis. It includes company information, investment comparison, and analysis of share price data to support basic financial decision-making and investment evaluation.

### Files Included

* **Company Information.xlsx** – Company identification and related information.
* **Share price analysis-Investment comparison Report.xlsx** – Share price analysis and investment comparison report.
